THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Scientist by training.
Curious by nature.

I'm drawn to difficult problems that live between fundamental physics, computational methods, and real-world engineering.

MY BACKGROUND

Where the interests meet.

My background is in geophysics, with a focus on seismic data processing and imaging, wave propagation, and the numerical methods that make subsurface problems tractable.

Over time, that has extended naturally into high-performance computing, scientific software, and machine learning—not as separate pursuits, but as complementary ways to understand and solve complex problems.

I'm particularly interested in the bridge between mathematical ideas and useful systems: taking a model or algorithm beyond a derivation and into a workflow that others can trust and use.

HOW I APPROACH PROBLEMS

Three recurring principles.

01

Start with the physics.

Identify the governing assumptions and what the measurements can—and cannot—tell us.

02

Make the computation trustworthy.

Care about numerical accuracy, stability, reproducibility, and performance from the start.

03

Make knowledge transferable.

Document ideas clearly, build reusable tools, and connect technical decisions to human impact.
